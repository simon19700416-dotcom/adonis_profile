技术特点与提升重点 Playing Profile & Development Focus

优势环节 Strengths

沙坑救球果断、落点与滚动控制稳定；9 号铁与 8 号铁弹道重复性最高（偏离极少超过 6–7 码）；5 号木可控性优于 3 号木，右侧无破坏性失误；攻果岭前有先看坡度再决定打旗前或果岭中心的习惯。

Decisive bunker play with reliable distance-and-roll control; most repeatable ball flight with the 9- and 8-iron (rarely more than 6–7 yards offline); the 5-wood is more controllable than the 3-wood with no destructive miss to the right; reads green slopes before choosing between a pin-side and centre-green target.

提升重点 Development Focus

中长推的读线与速度控制（增加侧向看线）；开球瞄准趋于保守（瞄准球道中央或略偏左）；障碍球位的距离判断需实际演练；比赛中的情绪转换与节奏管理；耐力与营养补充。

Lag-putting line reading and speed control (adding a side-on read); more conservative driver targets (centre or slightly left of the fairway); practical rehearsal of distance judgement from trouble lies; emotional reset and pace between shots; endurance and in-round nutrition.
